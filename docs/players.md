# Worlds and portals

Pixel Retreat has more than one world on each server: the normal overworld, the Nether, the End,
and extra worlds such as event areas. Each world can have its own rules, so what you can do
depends on where you are.

## Getting around

Atlas gives you travel and world-rule messages in your chosen language. Named destinations
are highlighted so you can identify the world or server involved, and suggested actions
use green text. You interact with portals directly; there is no Atlas menu to open.

### Portals

Some places have portals that take you to another world, to a fixed spot, or even to the other
server (Europe or North America). Walk into the portal to travel. You do not need a command.

- After using a portal you have to wait a moment (usually a few seconds) before a portal works
  for you again. This stops you from bouncing back and forth.
- Some portals are reserved for certain players or events. If nothing happens when you walk in,
  you may not have access to that portal yet.
- A portal to the other server moves you there through the network. Your trip finishes when you
  arrive; if it does not, you stay where you joined and can simply try again.

Portal messages tell you what happened:

- **"Off we go! Travelling to ..."** means the portal is sending you to the other server.
- **"Oops! ... isn't loaded right now. This portal can't take you there."** means the
  destination world is unavailable. Contact staff if you need help reaching it.
- **"Whoops, the portal hiccupped! Give it another try."** means the portal could not
  send you. Try again, and contact staff if it keeps happening.
- **"Your portal trip hit a bump! You're staying right where you joined."** means the
  trip could not be completed on arrival. You remain at your joining position.

## World rules

Each world decides things such as:

- whether monsters or animals appear;
- whether players can hurt each other (PvP);
- whether you can break or place blocks;
- whether you take fall, fire, explosion or drowning damage, and whether you get hungry;
- whether you can drop or pick up items, and whether you keep your inventory when you die;
- whether you can trade items with other players;
- the time of day and the weather, and whether sleeping skips the night;
- your game mode: some worlds put everyone in, for example, adventure mode.

These rules are set by staff and apply to everybody in that world. They change when you move to
another world.

## FAQ

**Why is a destination world temporarily unavailable?**
Some world changes need a preparation start followed by an activation start.
During that period a portal destination can be unavailable. Ask staff when it
will reopen; you do not need to change your settings or run a command.

**I can't break or place blocks here.**
Building is turned off in this world. Go to a world where building is allowed, such as the
survival world.

**I can't hurt another player.**
PvP is turned off in this world.

**My game mode changed when I entered a world.**
That world has a fixed game mode. You get the normal game mode back when you leave.

**It's always day (or never rains) in this world.**
Some worlds have a fixed time or weather. Sleeping cannot change it there.

**I can't trade with another player.**
Trading is on by default, but a world's rules can turn it off. If the game tells you trading is
not allowed here, move to a world where trading is allowed and try again. If your trade stops for
another reason, follow the message the trade gives you, and contact staff if it keeps happening.

**I walked into a portal and nothing happened.**
Wait a few seconds and try again; portals have a short waiting time. If it still does nothing,
the portal may be reserved, or its destination may be closed for the moment.

**A portal said my trip could not be completed.**
Try the portal again. If it keeps happening, tell a staff member which portal you used.

**There are no monsters here at all.**
Monsters are turned off in this world. That is on purpose.
